PALM COVE

Courier Vera Solano — or her partner Dario “Dax” Vega — owes fixer Lucia Marrero, and Palm Cove collects its debts in cash, cars and close calls. A low-poly open-world crime game on a long Florida peninsula at dusk. New Game wakes you up at home.

WASD move / drive · Mouse drag orbits the camera (it follows behind you as you turn) · Shift sprint · E enter car / building / shop · F punch / use · Click fire · 1–5 hotbar · X use item · Right-mouse/V sniper scope · R race · T taxi · B bail · Esc/P pause

CHOOSE YOUR LEAD

Two couriers, one debt. The partner you don't pick still rides with Lucia's crew — you'll meet them at the garage. Whoever you pick wakes up in the same Coral Heights home.

VERA SOLANO — blonde ponytail, pink tank, ripped jeans. The courier who owes the debt.
DARIO “DAX” VEGA — spiky hair, floral shirt, gold chain, pink trousers. Her partner, the getaway driver.
